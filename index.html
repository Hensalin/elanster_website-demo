FAQ

Common questions

How fast can you deliver an AI MVP?

Most AI MVPs ship in 4 to 12 weeks from kickoff to production — not a prototype, but a deployed product on real infrastructure. Timelines depend on scope; we’ll give you an honest estimate on the strategy call.

What AI research services do you offer?

Full-stack AI products end to end: AI research, AI integration into existing products, autonomous agents and workflow automation, RAG pipelines, and AI consulting — all backed by web, mobile, and cloud engineering.

Which industries do you work with?

We’ve delivered across e-commerce, healthcare, faith-based organizations, human resources, education, professional services, and retail. Strong engineering fundamentals travel well between domains.

What makes you different from other AI development agencies?

We’re not an AI shop that learned engineering last year — we’re a product engineering company that builds AI properly. Architecture first, humans in the loop, production-ready from day one, and accountable long after launch.

How do your engagements work?

We start with a strategy call to understand your goals, constraints, and timeline. From there we map the right model — a fixed-scope build, an embedded product team, or ongoing partnership — with two meetings a week and full visibility throughout.

Do you work with larger companies?

Yes, and smaller ones too. Company-grade delivery is our default standard — security protocols, code review discipline, and compliance-aware architecture apply to every project, whatever its size.
