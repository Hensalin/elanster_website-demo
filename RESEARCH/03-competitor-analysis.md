## Patterns across the set
1. **Every one shows named proof** (logos, metrics or testimonials). Elanster shows none. This is the largest gap.
2. **Entry offers beyond a call**: readiness assessments (Aubergine, Intellectsoft), estimators (Netguru), free tools/demos (thoughtbot, STX Next), gated reports.
3. **Stated response times** (GeekyAnts, Intellectsoft) reduce hesitation.
4. **Named engagement models with durations** (GeekyAnts) help buyers self-qualify.
5. **Specific stack details and certifications** appear on most.
6. Few use heavy 3D; the memorable ones (ustwo, Fueled) rely on strong brand and confident type, not effects.

## Where Elanster can stand out (supported by its real facts)
- **Specific timeline promise** ("4 to 12 weeks, deployed, not a prototype") is sharper than most competitors' language.
- **Two-meeting cadence** (Monday planning, Friday review) is a concrete, easy-to-picture process.
- **Humans-in-the-loop, architecture-first** stance is a credible counter to AI hype.
- **8 years of product engineering before AI**, honest about not being a new AI shop.
- The animated site is already more distinctive than most of the above; the gap is proof, not polish.

## Opportunities not yet taken
Readiness-review landing page; published follow-up timeline after a call (e.g. "notes within 24 hours" already appears in the site's contact copy); named engagement models with durations; stack page.

## What round 2 adds
1. **Candour sells.** Vstorm's free session "may conclude no" and the site repeatedly says waiting can be right. Bacancy ends its call with a written recommendation. This costs nothing and needs no logos.
2. **Ownership at handover** (Vstorm, Bacancy) is a trust lever. Elanster's site does not state code/IP ownership terms. Needs a business decision before any claim.
3. **Production vs proof-of-concept** is the shared pain (Addepto, Vstorm, Simform). Elanster already says "not a prototype."
4. **Entry offers beat "book a call" alone** (readiness assessments, free mapping sessions, written recommendations).
5. **Hype claims are weak:** "15 days", "#1 in 2026", inconsistent counts (Hyperlink, Pentalog) read as unreliable. Elanster's measured tone is an asset.
6. The weakest sites show no proof and no offer (Pentalog, Tryolabs call-only): Elanster's gap is proof, not positioning.
