FAQ

About how we work

How fast can you deliver an AI MVP?+

Most AI MVPs ship in 4 to 12 weeks from kickoff to production — not a prototype, but a deployed product on real infrastructure. Timelines depend on scope; we’ll give you an honest estimate on the strategy call.

How do your engagements work?+

We start with a strategy call to understand your goals, constraints, and timeline. From there we map the right model — a fixed-scope build, an embedded product team, or ongoing partnership — with two meetings a week and full visibility throughout.

Do you work with enterprise clients?+

Yes. Enterprise delivery is our default standard — security protocols, code review discipline, and compliance-aware architecture apply to every project, whatever its size.
