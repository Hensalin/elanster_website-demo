# 11 · Where words changed, and why

Review lens: a visitor skims for five seconds, then looks for proof, then wants a low-risk next step.

| Where | Before | After | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero body | "Mission-critical AI that powers today's enterprises and tomorrow's breakthroughs, built on eight years..." | "We build production AI products, from document search to agents to workflow automation, and deploy them in 4 to 12 weeks. Senior engineers, two meetings a week, accountable after launch." | The old line was abstract. The new one says what, how fast and how. |
| Hero actions | Two buttons | Added "Not sure where to start? Check your project fit →" | Lower-commitment path in the first screen |
| Nav, buttons, all pages | "Discovery Call" / "Strategy Call" / "Schedule a Discovery Call" mixed | "Strategy Call" everywhere | One name for one action; clearer |
| About button | "Learn more about us" (went to Services) | "See what we build" | Label matched the destination |
| New band after About | none | "How you can judge us before you sign anything" (4 checkable promises) | Proof by process while client proof is unavailable |
| Work heading | "Representative engagements" | "The problems we get hired to solve" + honest sub-line (illustrative, not client results) | Pain-first and honest |
| Document Intelligence card | "80% less manual review." (unverified) | "Less manual review, faster routing." | Removed unverified statistic (home and Work page) |
| Project-fit section | "Not sure where to start?" | "Not sure AI is the right answer yet? Start here." | Speaks to the real doubt |
| FAQ | 6 questions | 10: added "What happens on the strategy call?", "What does production-ready mean?", "What if AI isn't the right answer?", "Where is Elanster based?" | Answers real objections; feeds AI answer engines. Home, FAQ schema and script updated together |
