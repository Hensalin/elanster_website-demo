8 years of web, mobile and cloud product engineering · AI-enabled development, integration, agents and workflow automation, RAG, consulting · AI MVPs deployed in 4 to 12 weeks · two meetings a week · security protocols and code review on every project · Chennai, serving globally · unit of ED Creative Group.

## Messaging rules
Use concrete, checkable claims ("4 to 12 weeks", "two meetings a week"). Avoid "revolutionise", "limitless", "next-generation". Present illustrative work as illustrative. Never state a statistic that isn't verified.

## Suggested headline options (hero H1 stays "Engineered to last. Designed to lead." unless you choose otherwise)
Add a clearer sub-line under it, e.g. "Production AI products, deployed in 4 to 12 weeks." (hero body copy already says similar).
