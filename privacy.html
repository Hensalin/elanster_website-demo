Who we are

Elanster Technologies, a unit of ED Creative Group, based in Chennai, India. This notice explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you contact us or book a call we collect the details you share, such as your name, email address, company and message. Our website may also record basic technical information such as your browser type and pages visited, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss a possible project with you. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last conversation, then delete them, unless we are working together and need them for that work.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to host this website and our systems (for example Vercel and Supabase). They process data only on our instructions.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
